\subsubsection{Forward Volatility Agreement}
\label{input:forwardvolatilityagreement}

\ifdefined\IncludePayoff{{\bf Payoff}

A Forward Volatility Agreement (FVA) is a contract between two counterparties that agrees on the implied volatility of a
forward-starting option. The trade supports two payoff types: \emph{Straddle} (default) and \emph{Put}.

\textbf{Straddle payoff.} The long party pays a fixed premium computed as the sum of a call and a put with the
aggreed implied volatility strike, and in return receives the absolute payoff of the forward-starting straddle.

The fixed premium paid at the premium date is:

$$
\text{fixedPremium} = \text{Black}(\text{call}, F, K, \sigma, \tau) + \text{Black}(\text{put}, F, K, \sigma, \tau)
$$

where $F$ is the forward price of the underlying at the FVA date for expiry at the option expiry date, $K$ is the
option strike (defaults to $F$ if not specified), $\sigma$ is the agreed implied volatility strike, and $\tau$ is the
time from the FVA date to option expiry.

The floating payoff received at the settlement date is:

$$
\text{floatingPayoff} = |K - S(T)|
$$

where $S(T)$ is the spot price of the underlying at option expiry $T$.

The Straddle NPV from the perspective of the long party is:

\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
\text{FVA NPV}
= \text{LongShort} \cdot \text{Quantity} \cdot \Big(
&\text{PAY}(\text{floatingPayoff},\, T,\, T_{\text{settle}},\, \text{PayCcy}) \\
&{}- \text{PAY}(\text{fixedPremium},\, T_{\text{FVA}},\, T_{\text{premium}},\, \text{PayCcy})
\Big)
\end{split}
\end{equation}

\textbf{Put payoff.} The long party pays a fixed premium equal to the Black-Scholes value of a put option at the
FVA date, computed using the spot price of the underlying at the FVA date, and in return receives the intrinsic
put payoff at option expiry.

The fixed premium paid at the premium date is the Black-Scholes put price:

$$
\text{fixedPremium} = K \cdot e^{-r\tau} N(-d_2) - S_0 \cdot e^{-q\tau} N(-d_1)
$$

where $S_0 = S(T_{\text{FVA}})$ is the spot at the FVA date, $K$ is the option strike, $r$ is the fixed (risk-free)
rate, $q$ is the dividend yield, and

$$
d_1 = \frac{\ln(S_0/K) + (r - q + \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2)\tau}{\sigma\sqrt{\tau}}, \qquad
d_2 = \frac{\ln(S_0/K) + (r - q - \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2)\tau}{\sigma\sqrt{\tau}}
$$

The floating payoff received at the settlement date is:

$$
\text{Payoff} = \max(K - S(T),\, 0)
$$

The Put NPV from the perspective of the long party is:

\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
\text{FVA NPV}
= \text{LongShort} \cdot \text{Quantity} \cdot \Big(
&\text{PAY}(\text{Payoff},\, T,\, T_{\text{settle}},\, \text{PayCcy}) \\
&{}- \text{PAY}(\text{fixedPremium},\, T_{\text{FVA}},\, T_{\text{premium}},\, \text{PayCcy})
\Big)
\end{split}
\end{equation}

{\bf Input}}\fi

A Forward Volatility Agreement is set up using trade
type \emph{FxForwardVolatilityAgreement}, \emph{EquityForwardVolatilityAgreement} or \emph{CommodityForwardVolatilityAgreement},
depending on the underlying asset class, and an associated
\lstinline!FxForwardVolatilityAgreementData!, \lstinline!EquityForwardVolatilityAgreementData! or \lstinline!CommodityForwardVolatilityAgreementData! node,
as shown in Listing \ref{lst:fva_data}. The node elements have the following meaning:

\begin{itemize}
\item FvaDate: The date on which the forward option strike is fixed and the fixed premium is observed. This is also
  the start of the forward period.

  Allowable values: See \lstinline!Date! in Table \ref{tab:allow_stand_data}.

\item OptionExpiry: The expiry date of the forward-starting option. The floating payoff is determined by the spot
  price of the underlying on this date.

  Allowable values: See \lstinline!Date! in Table \ref{tab:allow_stand_data}. Must be strictly after \lstinline!FvaDate!.

\item PremiumDate: The date on which the fixed premium is paid. Must be on or after \lstinline!FvaDate!.

  Allowable values: See \lstinline!Date! in Table \ref{tab:allow_stand_data}.

\item Underlying: The underlying asset definition. The ordering of FX currency pairs defines the observed value, i.e.
  for \lstinline!ECB-GBP-USD! the domestic currency is USD (the value is expressed in USD per GBP).

  Allowable values: See \ref{ss:underlying}.

\item LongShort: Whether the party is long or short the forward volatility agreement.

  Allowable values: \emph{Long}, \emph{Short}.

\item UnderlyingStrike [Optional]: The strike of the forward-starting option, typically agreed at the at-the-money
  forward level on trade date. If omitted, the strike defaults at valuation time to the forward price of the underlying
  at the FVA date for option expiry (i.e.\ it is set to the at-the-money forward level consistent with the model).

  Allowable values: Any positive real number.

\item ImpliedVolStrike: The pre-agreed annualised implied volatility used to compute the fixed premium. Expressed in
  absolute (decimal) terms, e.g. \lstinline!0.09675! for 9.675\%.

  Allowable values: Any positive real number.

\item Quantity: The notional quantity of the agreement.

  Allowable values: Any non-negative real number.

\item PayCcy: The currency in which the fixed premium and the floating payoff are settled.

  Allowable values: See Table \ref{tab:currency} \lstinline!Currency!.

\item SettlementDate: The date on which the floating payoff is settled. Must be on or after \lstinline!OptionExpiry!.

  Allowable values: See \lstinline!Date! in Table \ref{tab:allow_stand_data}.

\item PayoffType [Optional]: The payoff type of the FVA. Defaults to \emph{Straddle} if omitted.

  \begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Straddle}: The fixed premium is the sum of a call and a put (Black formula), and the floating payoff
    is $|K - S(T)|$.
  \item \emph{Put}: The fixed premium is the Black-Scholes put price computed using the spot at the FVA date, and
    the floating payoff is $\max(K - S(T), 0)$. \lstinline!UnderlyingStrike!, \lstinline!DayCountFraction!,
    \lstinline!DividendYield!, and \lstinline!FixedRate! are all required when this type is selected.
  \end{itemize}

  Allowable values: \emph{Straddle}, \emph{Put}.

\item DayCountFraction [Optional]: The day count convention used to compute the time fraction $\tau$ from
  \lstinline!FvaDate! to \lstinline!OptionExpiry! in the Black-Scholes premium calculation. Required when
  \lstinline!PayoffType! is \emph{Put}.

  Allowable values: See Table \ref{tab:daycount} Day Count Fraction.

\item DividendYield [Optional]: The continuous dividend yield $q$ used in the Black-Scholes premium calculation.
  Required when \lstinline!PayoffType! is \emph{Put}. Expressed in decimal form, e.g.\ \lstinline!0.02! for 2\%.

  Allowable values: Any real number.

\item FixedRate [Optional]: The continuous risk-free rate $r$ used in the Black-Scholes premium calculation.
  Required when \lstinline!PayoffType! is \emph{Put}. Expressed in decimal form, e.g.\ \lstinline!0.05! for 5\%.

  Allowable values: Any real number.
\end{itemize}
