The quote format is:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \lstinline!SHAPE_PROFILE/SHAPE_FACTOR/<QuoteName>/<DeliveryDate>/<StartTime>/<Unit>[/DST]!
\end{itemize}

Examples:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \lstinline!SHAPE_PROFILE/SHAPE_FACTOR/PJM_WH_RT_PK_HOURLY/2021-01-01/0/SEC!
  \item \lstinline!SHAPE_PROFILE/SHAPE_FACTOR/PJM_WH_RT_PK_HOURLY/2021-01-01/3600/SEC!
  \item \lstinline!SHAPE_PROFILE/SHAPE_FACTOR/PJM_WH_RT_PK_HOURLY/2021-01-01/7200/SEC!
  \item \lstinline!SHAPE_PROFILE/SHAPE_FACTOR/PJM_WH_RT_PK_HOURLY/2021-03-01/7200/SEC/DST!
\end{itemize}

The quote value is always a price in currency per MWh during the delivery window.

Assume for illustration that these four quotes represent prices in USD/MWh for each delivery window. The first quote
is for 00:00--01:00, the second for 01:00--02:00 and the third for 02:00 onward according to the profile definition.
The last quote with the optional \lstinline!DST! flag is only used on a backward DST day for the extra 02:00--03:00
hour.

For backward DST handling, there must be a \lstinline!DST! quote starting at 02:00 and all \lstinline!DST! quotes for
the day must have a start time strictly before 03:00.

Worked examples:
\begin{itemize}
\item Assume the daily average price is 100 USD/MWh, the shape factor for
  \lstinline!SHAPE_PROFILE/SHAPE_FACTOR/PJM_WH_RT_PK_HOURLY/2021-01-01/3600/SEC! is 1.10 and the contract delivers
  40 MW from 01:15 to 01:30. The delivered energy is
$$
40 \times \frac{15}{60} = 10 \text{ MWh}
$$
and the delivery falls fully into the 01:00--02:00 bucket, so the price is
$$
100 \times 1.10 = 110 \text{ USD/MWh}.
$$

\item Assume the daily average price is again 100 USD/MWh, the 01:00--02:00 shape factor is 1.10 and the 02:00 onward
  shape factor from \lstinline!SHAPE_PROFILE/SHAPE_FACTOR/PJM_WH_RT_PK_HOURLY/2021-01-01/7200/SEC! is 1.00. If the
  contract delivers 50 MW from 01:30 to 02:30, the first half hour contributes 25 MWh in the 01:00--02:00 bucket and
  the second half hour contributes 25 MWh in the 02:00--03:00 bucket. The weighted price is
$$
100 \times \frac{25 \times 1.10 + 25 \times 1.0}{50} = 105 \text{ USD/MWh}.
$$
\end{itemize}
